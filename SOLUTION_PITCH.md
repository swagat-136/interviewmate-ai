# InterviewMate AI — Solution Pitch

## Practice smarter. Interview with confidence.

### The problem
Many students and early-career job seekers struggle to prepare for interviews in a structured way. They often lack access to realistic mock interviews, role-focused practice questions, and clear feedback on how to improve their answers. Language and confidence barriers can make preparation even harder.

### Our solution
**InterviewMate AI** is an AI-assisted interview preparation platform that helps candidates practise interview scenarios, respond to questions, and review their performance in one place. It is designed to make interview preparation more accessible, structured, and repeatable.

### How it helps
- **Role-focused practice:** Candidates can prepare for different job roles using targeted interview questions.
- **Voice-based responses:** The platform is designed to accept spoken answers, with speech-to-text support through the Gnani integration.
- **Structured feedback:** Answers can be reviewed against criteria such as relevance, clarity, completeness, and technical accuracy.
- **Progress review:** Interview sessions and feedback can help candidates identify strengths and areas to improve.
- **Accessible experience:** A web-based interface supports a convenient practice workflow.

### Intended users
Students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals preparing for technical, behavioural, and general job interviews.

### Technology
- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Speech integration:** Gnani speech-to-text API
- **Supporting tools:** React Router and Python-based evaluation services

### Expected impact
InterviewMate AI aims to give candidates a practical way to rehearse interviews, understand the quality of their responses, and practise more consistently—helping them approach real interviews with greater preparation and confidence.

### Project status
This repository contains the project's implementation and supporting services. Some integrations and deployment steps may still require configuration, including valid API credentials and a hosted backend. The pitch describes the intended solution and current project direction, not a claim that every feature is production-ready.
